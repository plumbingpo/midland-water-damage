Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Midland Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Midland and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Midland

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
